Aether song

An Aether instrument for dark ambient source. Long mournful cries, dry or with echo or hall, mono or stereo. Download a 24-bit / 48 kHz PCM WAV for the DAW.

Idle. Seed 1971 recalls the Payne & McVay song paper.
Format 24-bit PCM Rate 48 kHz Channels stereo Structure —

Mournful cries are the classic signature: a tone rises a little, holds, then falls over four to ten seconds, mostly between 150 and 700 Hz, with a slow quiver and a long silence after. Blue whale moans sit at 14–28 Hz; the audible mode pitches them up so you can hear the same shape. Depth darkens the tone. Octave shifts the whole song. Space can be off, a short echo, or the hall. Mono is a true one-channel file. Live plays the next seed while this one sounds, so a session can run for hours without building a giant file.
